01 / ABOUT

Research-led teaching.
Impactful innovation.

Dr Anika Kanwal is a Computer Science academic with over a decade of teaching experience across Australia and Pakistan, including Swinburne University of Technology and the University of Adelaide. She teaches programming, artificial intelligence, data structures, software development and business analytics.

Her research examines neural-network models for stock price prediction and image classification. She received Swinburne's 2023 Vice-Chancellor’s Reconciliation Award for empowering Indigenous perspectives in STEM.
